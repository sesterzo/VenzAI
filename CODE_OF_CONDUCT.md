# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and respectful. Disagree on the technical merits. Keep the
conversation on the work.

## What this applies to

Issues, pull requests, and any other interaction in this repository.

## What is expected

- Describe problems accurately. Include the log, the build number, and the
  steps that reproduce it.
- Disagree on substance, not on people. A patch can be wrong; the person who
  sent it is not.
- Accept that the author makes the final call on what fits the design. "I
  disagree with this decision" is a legitimate position; continuing to argue
  after it is made is not a good use of anyone's time.

## What is not welcome

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissive language.
- Demands for features or timelines.
- Pressure to change the licence, the pricing, or the terms.

## Reporting

If something in this repository makes the space feel unsafe or unwelcoming,
use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: **Security → Report a
vulnerability** on this repository's page. Reports are handled
confidentially.
